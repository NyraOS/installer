  One more step before it starts. A blue screen asks you to trust the Nyra      
  key. It appears right after the restart and waits only 10 seconds.            
                                                                                
  1. In UTM, remove the installer disc: open the CD/DVD drive menu and          
     choose “Eject”. Click the UTM window so it gets your keyboard.             
  2. Restart. When the blue screen says “Press any key”, press a key.           
  3. Choose “Enroll MOK”.                                                       
  4. Choose “View key 0” and compare the name and fingerprint with the ones     
     below. Then choose “Continue”.                                             
  5. Choose “Continue”, then “Yes”.                                             
  6. Type the one-time password you wrote down, without the dash and with       
     Caps Lock off. Then choose “Reboot”.
